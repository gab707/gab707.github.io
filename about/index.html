Gabriel
Kocher

Gabriel Kocher, known professionally as Gab707, is a Swiss-Canadian aerial cinematographer and Director of Photography based in Vancouver. He works at the point where aerial engineering and sports cinematography meet, capturing dynamic movement through bespoke first-person view drone systems.

He came to cinematography through competitive drone racing, where he became Canada’s most decorated pilot. That flying background is why the aircraft and the camera work are inseparable in his practice: the machine is designed around the movement it has to hold.

His work has appeared in major sports broadcasts, adventure films and commercial productions, with nominations at the NY Drone Film Festival and for Sports Emmy Awards.
